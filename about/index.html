Our Services Work With Us

Who we are

One team, end-to-end

Founded in 2022, BETA Social (operating as BETA Communications Sdn. Bhd.) is a full-service marketing agency based in Petaling Jaya, Selangor. We work with brands across Malaysia — from social media and content to paid media, websites, and experiential events.

Our philosophy is simple: strategy first, execution second. Every engagement starts with understanding your business and audience, then moves through creative production, distribution, and continuous optimisation. Because we handle the full spectrum in-house, the work stays coherent — your social, your ads, your website, and your events all tell the same story.
